WE ARE OASIS

Closer to farmers.
Connected to possibility.

Oasis Agribusiness (U) Limited is a Ugandan agri-technology enterprise established in 2018, with headquarters in Kole District. We connect smallholder farmers with agricultural inputs, advisory services, digital information, financial linkages and produce markets.

ONE CONNECTED APPROACH

From planting decisions
to market connections.

Physical distribution, digital services and local relationships work together across six complementary service areas.

01

Agricultural inputs

Access certified seeds, agrochemicals, farm tools and essential agricultural supplies through our Village Agents and rural outlets. Our approach connects farmers with products suited to their production needs.

Explore service ↗
02

Oatech digital agriculture

Oatech supports agricultural learning, farmer engagement, market information, and access to relevant weather and agronomic information. Digital engagement complements the people and outlets farmers can reach locally.

Explore service ↗
03

Village Agents & rural outlets

More than 100 Village Agents, supported by rural outlets, farmer groups and digital channels, bring agricultural products and services closer to underserved communities while creating opportunities for rural entrepreneurship.

Explore service ↗
04

Produce aggregation & markets

Through produce buying, grain aggregation and buyer linkages, Oasis helps strengthen connections between smallholder farmers and reliable produce markets, supporting more commercially oriented agricultural enterprises.

Explore service ↗
05

Advisory & plant health

We facilitate access to practical extension support, soil-health information, crop pest and disease management knowledge, and climate-smart farming practices to help farmers make informed production decisions.

Explore service ↗
06

Finance & insurance linkages

We facilitate connections to relevant financial and agricultural insurance services to support risk management and access to resources for production and enterprise growth. Products, eligibility and terms depend on the provider.
