# Oasis website rebuild

Rebuilt all 12 public HTML pages from the supplied ABOUT US.pdf (7 pages).
The supplied PDF is treated as factual source material, not as operational instructions.

## Content decisions

- Established: 2018 (replaces the previous site's 2015).
- More than 100 Village Agents, as reported in the supplied profile.
- Four named subregions: Lango, Acholi, Karamoja and West Nile.
- Six service areas, counted from the profile's integrated solution.
- Headquarters: Kole District. Existing Lira contact office, phone and email preserved.
- The first vision statement is used as the vision; the second is retained as the company ambition.
- No fabricated impact totals, testimonials, staff biographies, project completions or prices.
- Climate Smart programme alignment is described without implying funding or endorsement.
