What is Oasis Agribusiness?

Oasis Agribusiness (U) Limited is a Ugandan agri-technology enterprise established in 2018. It connects smallholder farmers with agricultural inputs, digital information, advisory services, financial linkages and produce markets.

Where does Oasis operate?

Our activities extend across Northern Uganda, including Lango, Acholi, Karamoja and West Nile. Our headquarters are in Kole District. The contact office listed on this website is in Lira City.

How large is the Village Agent network?

The supplied company profile reports more than 100 Village Agents, supported by rural outlets, farmer groups and digital channels.

What does Oatech do?

Oatech supports agricultural learning, farmer engagement, market information and access to relevant weather and agronomic information. Contact our team for current access details.

Can I buy seeds and agricultural inputs?

Oasis facilitates access to certified seeds, agrochemicals, farm tools and other agricultural supplies. Contact the team to confirm products, stock, suitability and prices.

Does Oasis provide loans or insurance directly?

The profile describes financial and insurance linkages. We facilitate connections to relevant providers; availability, eligibility and terms must be confirmed with the provider.

Does Oasis buy agricultural produce?

Produce buying, grain aggregation and buyer linkages are part of our business model. Contact the office to discuss your crop, quantity, location and current opportunities.

How can I partner with Oasis?

We welcome discussions with farmers, input suppliers, produce buyers, financial institutions, technology providers, government institutions and development partners. Call or email the office to introduce your proposal.
