ABOUT US
Who We Are
Oasis Agribusiness (U) Limited is a Ugandan agri-technology enterprise
established in 2018, with its headquarters in Kole District, Northern Uganda. We
are committed to transforming agricultural service delivery by connecting
smallholder farmers to quality agricultural inputs, digital agricultural
information, advisory services, financial linkages, and reliable produce
markets.
Our business model integrates a community-based network of more than 100
Village Agents and rural outlets with our Oatech digital platform. Through this
hybrid approach, we bring essential agricultural products, knowledge, and
services closer to underserved farming communities, including women, young
people, refugees, and host communities.
We operate across Northern Uganda, including the Lango, Acholi, Karamoja,
and West Nile subregions, with a focus on strengthening agricultural
productivity, improving farmer incomes, supporting rural entrepreneurship, and
building climate-resilient agricultural value chains.
The Challenge We Address
Agriculture remains a major source of livelihoods and employment in Uganda.
However, smallholder farmers and agricultural enterprises in Northern Uganda
face persistent barriers that limit productivity, commercial growth, and job
creation.
These challenges include limited access to affordable, quality agricultural
inputs; inadequate working capital; high transportation and distribution costs;
unreliable market access; limited access to timely weather and agronomic
information; and insufficient access to practical agricultural advisory services.
Climate change compounds these challenges through unpredictable rainfall,
prolonged dry spells, drought, pests, and crop diseases. Unsustainable land
management and declining soil health can further undermine productivity
and weaken the resilience of farming households.
Women and young people are particularly affected by barriers to productive
resources, finance, skills, and profitable market opportunities. At the same time,
agricultural SMEs often struggle to access the working capital and investment
required to expand operations, strengthen supply chains, and create
sustainable employment.

Our Innovative Solution
We combines last-mile agricultural distribution, digital technology, extension
support, and market linkages to improve the efficiency and inclusiveness of
agricultural value chains.
Our integrated solution comprises:
I. Agricultural inputs and services- We facilitate access to certified seeds,
agrochemicals, farm tools, and other essential agricultural supplies that
help farmers meet their production needs.
II. Digital agriculture through Oatech- Our digital platform supports
agricultural learning, farmer engagement, market information, and
access to relevant weather and agronomic information.
III. Village Agents and rural outlets- Our community-based distribution
network brings agricultural products and services closer to farmers while
creating opportunities for local entrepreneurship and employment.
IV. Produce aggregation and market access-Through produce buying,
grain aggregation, and buyer linkages, we support agricultural
commercialisation and strengthen connections between farmers and
markets.
V. Agricultural advisory and plant health support- We facilitate access to
practical extension services, soil-health information, crop pest and
disease management knowledge, and climate-smart farming practices.
VI. Financial and insurance linkages- We facilitate connections to relevant
agricultural insurance and financial services to support risk management
and improve access to resources for agricultural production and
enterprise growth.
By integrating physical distribution with digital services and market
connections, we aims to reduce access barriers, improve operational
efficiency, and establish a scalable business model for underserved
agricultural communities.

Our Contribution to Climate-Smart Jobs
Oasis recognises that sustainable job creation in agriculture depends on
improving productivity, strengthening climate resilience, expanding markets,
and building commercially viable rural enterprises.
Our contribution focuses on three interconnected areas.
Climate-Smart Agriculture and Farmer Resilience
We promote access to drought-tolerant and early-maturing seeds, integrated
pest management practices, soil-health improvement methods, and timely
digital weather and agronomic information.
These interventions are intended to help farmers make informed production
decisions, manage climate-related risks, and improve the resilience of their
farming enterprises.
We also recognise the importance of sustainable land management in
protecting soil productivity and the natural resources on which agricultural
livelihoods depend. We seek to strengthen the promotion of responsible
farming practices through our distribution network, farmer engagement, and
partnerships.
Agricultural Commercialisation and Market Development
By connecting farmers to agricultural inputs, advisory services, produce
aggregation, and market opportunities, Oasis supports the development of
more commercially oriented farming enterprises.
Our grain aggregation and produce marketing activities help strengthen
market connections, while our Village Agent network supports the distribution
of agricultural products and services into rural communities.
As the business grows, we aim to strengthen supply chains, expand market
reach, improve service delivery, and increase opportunities for smallholder
farmers and agricultural SMEs to participate in profitable value chains.
Rural Employment and Entrepreneurship
Our Village Agent and rural outlet model creates opportunities for local
businesses to participate in agricultural input distribution, farmer engagement,
and service delivery.
Our operations also support employment and enterprise opportunities across
produce aggregation, logistics, agricultural extension, and digital agriculture.

Our Scale and Reach

Our Business Sustainability and Growth Potential
Oasis operates a diversified agribusiness model with revenue-generating
activities in agricultural input distribution, produce buying and grain
aggregation, and ICT-enabled services.

serve farmers through multiple channels while building relationships across

With suitable investment and technical assistance, Oasis aims to strengthen its
supply chain, improve inventory availability, expand its rural distribution
network, increase produce aggregation capacity, and enhance the reach of

These investments would support business growth while creating opportunities
for additional rural enterprises, agricultural workers, and smallholder farmers.

Our Alignment with the Climate Smart Programme
Oasis's business model aligns with the Climate Smart programme's emphasis on
private-sector-led agricultural development, climate adaptation, inclusive
economic growth, and sustainable job creation.
Our contribution is centred on the practical delivery of agricultural products,
services, technology, and market linkages through a scalable rural distribution
network.
We see strong opportunities to contribute to programme objectives in the
following areas:
Climate resilience: Expanding farmers' access to climate-smart inputs,
agronomic information, and practices that support adaptation to changing
climatic conditions.
Private-sector growth: Strengthening an established agricultural SME that
connects input suppliers, farmers, produce buyers, and other value-chain
actors.
Inclusive employment: Expanding rural entrepreneurship and job opportunities
through Village Agents, outlets, aggregation, and agricultural services, with
particular attention to women and youth.
Agricultural commercialisation: Improving connections between production,
input distribution, aggregation, and produce markets.
Sustainable land management: Promoting soil-health improvement and
responsible agricultural practices through farmer engagement and strategic
partnerships.
Digital innovation: Extending the reach of agricultural learning, advisory
information, and market services through OaTech and community-based
distribution.
Through these activities, Oasis aims to contribute to a more productive,
inclusive, and climate-resilient agricultural economy in Northern Uganda.
