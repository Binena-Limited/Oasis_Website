# Oasis original template update

The public pages and assets were restored from the user's oasis_web.zip.
Original layout classes, colours, spacing, page sections, sliders, icons and responsive
rules are retained. The only intentional CSS changes concern text typography: Jost
replaces the previous text families and is self-hosted in weights 100 through 900.
Font Awesome remains for icon glyphs, not website text.

Content is based on the supplied ABOUT US.pdf. The website reports establishment
in 2018, more than 100 Village Agents, the four named Northern Uganda subregions,
and the six integrated service areas. Unsupported template impact statistics and
old programme details have been replaced with profile-supported information.
Existing contact details, including the Lira and Oyam contact locations, are retained.

New agricultural imagery is fitted to the original image dimensions. The image
manifest records original paths, replacement paths and dimensions. Images illustrate
areas of work; they do not document specific Oasis people or activities.

All 12 original page filenames remain. The current company profile download is
assets/documents/oasis-company-profile.pdf. Existing form behaviour is retained;
the template states that unconfigured forms do not send an enquiry and gives direct
phone and email details. No new back-end submission service is introduced.

Open index.html or upload the included files to the existing web host. The original
oasis_web.zip remains unchanged. The redesigned pages from the preceding attempt
are backed up in .oasis-work/before-template-restoration.
