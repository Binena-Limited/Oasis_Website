Agricultural support through agents, rural outlets and technology

Our agent and rural outlet model connects farmers with high-quality seeds, agro-chemicals, farm tools and agro insurance, alongside field-based support, electronic learning, financial linkages and enterprise development.

Oasis supports women, men, youth, refugees and host communities. Its company profile describes work across Lango, Karamoja, West Nile and Acholi.
