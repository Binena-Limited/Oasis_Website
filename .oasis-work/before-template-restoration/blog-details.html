RESOURCE 01

Access to inputs through local agents

More than 100 Village Agents, supported by rural outlets, farmer groups and digital channels, bring agricultural products and services closer to underserved communities while creating opportunities for rural entrepreneurship.

A local network can reduce the distance between a farmer and essential products or information. Contact Oasis to ask which service channels are relevant to your location.

RESOURCE 02

Digital knowledge through Oatech

Oatech supports agricultural learning, farmer engagement, market information, and access to relevant weather and agronomic information. Digital engagement complements the people and outlets farmers can reach locally.

Oatech supports learning, engagement and access to agricultural information. Contact Oasis for current platform access information and available support.

RESOURCE 03

Climate-smart farming and resilience

We facilitate access to practical extension support, soil-health information, crop pest and disease management knowledge, and climate-smart farming practices to help farmers make informed production decisions.

Our priorities include drought-tolerant and early-maturing seeds, integrated pest management, soil health and responsible land management. Seek advice suited to your crop, field conditions and season before choosing a specific intervention.

RESOURCE 04

Produce aggregation and market access

Through produce buying, grain aggregation and buyer linkages, Oasis helps strengthen connections between smallholder farmers and reliable produce markets, supporting more commercially oriented agricultural enterprises.

Produce buying and grain aggregation form part of our diversified business. Contact the team to discuss your produce, location and potential market connections.
