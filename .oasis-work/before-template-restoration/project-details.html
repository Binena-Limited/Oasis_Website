A practical approach to the climate and commercial challenges facing smallholder farmers and agricultural enterprises in Northern Uganda.

THE CHALLENGE

Changing conditions.
Connected challenges.

Unpredictable rainfall, prolonged dry spells, drought, pests and crop diseases can disrupt production. Limited access to quality inputs, working capital, timely information and reliable markets adds pressure to farming households.

Declining soil health and unsustainable land management can further weaken productivity. Women and young people also face barriers to finance, skills and productive resources.

OUR RESPONSE

Make practical support easier to reach.

We connect climate-smart inputs and agricultural knowledge with local distribution, digital engagement and market services. Sustainable land management and responsible farming practices are priorities for farmer engagement and partnerships.

Our approach aligns with the Climate Smart programme’s emphasis on private-sector-led development, climate adaptation, inclusive growth and sustainable jobs. This alignment describes our approach; it does not imply programme funding or endorsement.

OUR GROWTH PRIORITIES

Stronger supply chains.
More rural opportunity.

With suitable investment and technical assistance, we aim to improve inventory availability, strengthen logistics, expand rural distribution, increase aggregation capacity and enhance the reach of climate-smart services.
