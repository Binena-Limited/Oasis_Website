OUR APPROACH

A stronger rural economy.
One connection at a time.

Our model brings climate resilience, agricultural commercialisation and rural entrepreneurship together. These are our areas of work and growth, rather than a list of completed projects.

01 / CLIMATE-SMART AGRICULTURE

Help farms adapt.

We promote access to drought-tolerant and early-maturing seeds, integrated pest management, soil-health improvement methods and relevant weather information. These activities are intended to support informed decisions and more resilient farming enterprises.

Explore our approach ↗

02 / COMMERCIALISATION

Build stronger market connections.

Produce buying, grain aggregation and buyer linkages help connect production with markets. By bringing inputs, advice and aggregation together, we support commercially oriented farming and stronger rural supply chains.

03 / ENTREPRENEURSHIP

Create room for local enterprise.

Village Agents and rural outlets create opportunities in agricultural input distribution, farmer engagement and service delivery. Our operations also support enterprise opportunities in logistics, extension, produce aggregation and digital agriculture.

Women and young people are a particular focus as we work to expand opportunities through skills development, partnerships and business growth.
