WHO WE ARE

An agricultural enterprise.
A connected rural network.

Established in 2018, Oasis Agribusiness (U) Limited is a Ugandan agri-technology enterprise headquartered in Kole District, Northern Uganda. Our hybrid model combines more than 100 Village Agents and rural outlets with our Oatech digital platform.

We operate across Lango, Acholi, Karamoja and West Nile to strengthen productivity, support rural entrepreneurship and help build climate-resilient agricultural value chains.
